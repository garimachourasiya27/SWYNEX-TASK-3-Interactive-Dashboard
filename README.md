<h2 id="project-overview">📌 Project Overview</h2>

<p>
  The <strong>Cafe Sales Analytics Dashboard</strong> is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides insights into cafe sales performance throughout 2023 by analyzing revenue, transactions, quantity sold, product performance, payment methods, and locations.
</p>

<p>
  The dashboard helps users explore sales trends and understand performance through interactive visualizations and filters.
</p>

<p><strong>Reporting Period:</strong> January–December 2023</p>

<hr>

<h2 id="business-objectives">🎯 Business Objectives</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Monitor overall sales performance.</li>
  <li>Track total revenue and transaction volume.</li>
  <li>Analyze quantity sold across different items.</li>
  <li>Identify monthly revenue trends.</li>
  <li>Compare revenue across payment methods.</li>
  <li>Evaluate revenue performance by location.</li>
  <li>Analyze item-wise revenue and transaction distribution.</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h2 id="dashboard-pages">📊 Dashboard Pages</h2>

<h3>1. Cafe Sales Analytics Dashboard</h3>

<p>This page provides an overview of cafe sales performance through KPI cards, charts, and interactive slicers.</p>

<h4>Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)</h4>

<ul>
  <li>Total Revenue: 89.59K</li>
  <li>Total Transactions: 10K</li>
  <li>Total Quantity: 30K</li>
  <li>Average Transaction Value: 9</li>
</ul>

<h4>Visualizations</h4>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Revenue Trend Over Month:</strong> Analyzes revenue patterns throughout the year.</li>
  <li><strong>Quantity Sold by Item:</strong> Compares the quantity sold for each item.</li>
  <li><strong>Revenue by Payment Method:</strong> Compares revenue generated through different payment methods.</li>
  <li><strong>Revenue by Location:</strong> Evaluates revenue across locations.</li>
  <li><strong>Item-wise Revenue:</strong> Compares revenue generated by individual items.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Interactive Slicers</h4>

<ul>
  <li>Transaction Date</li>
  <li>Item</li>
  <li>Payment Method</li>
  <li>Location</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. Sales Insights</h3>

<p>This page focuses on monthly revenue performance, item-level analysis, payment methods, and transaction distribution.</p>

<h4>Visualizations</h4>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Highest Month Revenue:</strong> July 2023, approximately 11.1K.</li>
  <li><strong>Lowest Month Revenue:</strong> February 2023, approximately 6.7K.</li>
  <li><strong>Revenue Growth % by Item:</strong> Displays item-level values using a treemap visual.</li>
  <li><strong>Total Revenue by Payment Method:</strong> Compares revenue from Digital Wallet, Credit Card, and Cash.</li>
  <li><strong>Total Transactions by Item:</strong> Shows transaction distribution across items.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="key-insights">💡 Key Insights</h2>

<ul>
  <li>July 2023 recorded the highest monthly revenue.</li>
  <li>February 2023 recorded the lowest monthly revenue.</li>
  <li>Digital Wallet generated approximately 49K in revenue.</li>
  <li>Credit Card and Cash each generated approximately 21K in revenue.</li>
  <li>Item-level visualizations help compare product revenue, quantity sold, and transaction activity.</li>
  <li>Location and payment-method analysis provides additional perspectives on sales performance.</li>
</ul>
